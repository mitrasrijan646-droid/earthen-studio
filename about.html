Beginning

Alipi's introduction to design began at an early age.

Her curiosity about how spaces are built quietly became her own calling. What started as a course of study turned into a craft — years of training and freelancing that shaped her design instincts long before Earthen Studio existed. In 2019, that experience took shape as Earthen Studio.

Our Journey

Founded in 2019, Earthen Studio has led projects across new-build apartments and thoughtful revamps of older homes — each different in scale and story, same in spirit: complete, soothing, unmistakably considered. The studio moves fluently across styles — from pared-back contemporary interiors to opulent, heritage-inspired spaces layered with hand-painted murals, vintage silhouettes, and rich textures — shaping each home around what it calls for, not a fixed aesthetic.
